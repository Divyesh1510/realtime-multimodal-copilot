# Privacy Policy for AI Real-Time Interview & Meeting Copilot

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how the **AI Real-Time Interview & Meeting Copilot** Chrome Extension ("we", "our", or "the Extension") handles information when you use our software.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
- **Real-Time Processing Only:** The Extension processes user microphone audio and on-screen content (via tab capture or screen sharing) in real time solely to generate technical interview solutions, algorithmic explanations, and quiz answers.
- **No Data Retention:** No audio recordings, video streams, or screen captures are recorded, permanently stored, or retained on any server. Once the real-time inference is completed, the data is immediately discarded from memory.
- **No Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, IP address, browsing history, or physical location.

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.
- **Inference Providers:** Audio and screen chunks are processed via user-configured secure APIs (e.g., Groq, OpenRouter) exclusively to fulfill the single purpose of providing answers.

## 4. Local Settings
Any settings you configure within the extension (such as overlay transparency, preferred mode, or custom backend URL) are stored locally on your device via Chrome's `storage.local` API and never leave your browser.
